Played the cup? Your game log has every round. Drop it below, check that the result looks right, and send it. It gets processed within about 15 minutes whenever aizpun's PC is on, then published once aizpun has checked it.
  • The log is C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Zeepkist\BepInEx\LogOutput.log
  • The game overwrites it every time it starts, so grab it before relaunching Zeepkist.
  • Your file is read here in your browser first. Nothing is uploaded until you press Submit.
  • Regular cups only. Troll and Roulette cups are added by hand.
